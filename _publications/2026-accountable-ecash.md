This work proposes an accountable anonymous offline electronic
cash system based on BBS+ signatures and ElGamal encryption.

The system preserves payer and payee anonymity and transaction
unlinkability during normal operation. When illicit activity or
double spending is detected, it enables efficient forward and
backward selective tracing of the relevant coin flow.
