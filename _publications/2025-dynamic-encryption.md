This paper proposes a new encryption mechanism for efficiently
updating encrypted collaborative data.

The proposed bundled counter-mode structure allows only modified
portions of the ciphertext to be updated, avoiding the need to
decrypt and re-encrypt the entire dataset.

The work includes both security analysis and experimental
evaluation against traditional block cipher modes.
