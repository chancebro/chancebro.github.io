This paper proposes a practical privacy-preserving system for
surveillance videos that supports selective decryption.

The system assigns entity-specific identifiers and encryption keys
to detected facial regions. It allows only the regions associated
with a target entity to be decrypted while protecting unrelated
individuals.

A post-processing mechanism is also introduced to protect facial
regions missed by the recognition model.
