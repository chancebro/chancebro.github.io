# Research Vision

My research aims to develop privacy-preserving security mechanisms
for Physical AI and intelligent autonomous systems.

As robots become capable of perceiving, communicating, and acting
in human environments, they continuously process sensitive visual,
audio, location, and biometric information. My goal is to ensure
that these systems perform their intended functions without
unnecessarily exposing personal information.

Ultimately, I seek to create technical foundations for safe,
private, and accountable coexistence between humans and robots.

## Privacy for Physical AI

I study mechanisms for protecting sensitive information captured
by cameras, microphones, and sensors embedded in robots and
intelligent devices.

My research considers not only recognized information but also
privacy leakage caused by recognition failures, false negatives,
and uncertainty in AI perception.

## Privacy-Preserving Robot Authentication

Robots operating across multiple environments must verify each
other's permissions without exposing persistent identifiers.

I investigate anonymous credentials, zero-knowledge proofs,
revocation mechanisms, and conditional tracing for secure and
privacy-preserving robot authentication.

## Accountable Privacy

Privacy and accountability should not be treated as mutually
exclusive goals.

I design cryptographic systems that preserve anonymity and
unlinkability during normal operation while enabling narrowly
scoped identification or tracing when malicious behavior is
confirmed.

## Previous Research Foundations

My previous research includes:

- Selective encryption and decryption of privacy-sensitive regions
  in surveillance videos
- Efficient protection of dynamically updated encrypted data
- Accountable anonymous electronic cash with selective coin tracing
- Technical and regulatory analysis of stablecoins and AI agents
