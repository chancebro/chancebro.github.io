# Awards and Honors

## 2023

### Special Award, 2023 National Cryptography Competition

**Korea Cryptography Forum**  
October 2023

Received the Special Award in the 2023 National Cryptography
Competition in recognition of research and problem-solving
capabilities in cryptography and information security.

## 2022

### President’s Award, 2022 Side-Channel Information Analysis Competition

**Side-Channel Analysis Research Group, Korea Institute of
Information Security & Cryptology (KIISC)**  
Undergraduate Division  
October 2022

Received the President’s Award in the undergraduate division
for side-channel information analysis and security evaluation.

### Best Award, 2022 Security Idea Hackathon

**Dankook University Startup Support Center**  
October 2022

Received the Best Award for proposing an innovative and practical
information security idea.

### Bronze Award, Dankook University–Daou Technology Programming Competition

**Daou Technology and Dankook University**  
December 2022

Received the Bronze Award for programming performance and
technical problem-solving ability.
